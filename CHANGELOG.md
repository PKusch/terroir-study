# Changelog

What changed for someone studying with the app, newest first. The site updates
on every push to `main`, so dates are the day a change went live. Earlier work
is summed up in the README's roadmap.

## November 2026

### 26 November
- Small project files: a Node version file, an editor settings file, issue forms for something broken and for ideas, and time limits on the GitHub workflows.

### 25 November
- **Weak-spot map:** *Show my weak spots* on the Explore map shades each region by how often you answer its quiz questions right, and *Quiz this region only* starts a quiz on the region on screen.
- **Tell them apart:** a Taste drill on pairs of grapes that are easy to confuse, such as Merlot and Dornfelder.
- All 124 grape profiles checked again; seven small fixes, and a test that keeps winemaking aromas (butter, toast, cedar) out of a grape's own profile unless its notes explain them.

### 24 November
- The 122 questions added since 20 November checked again by a separate pass: no wrong answers, nine weak spots fixed.
- More questions that ask why, not just what.

### 22 to 23 November
- **Service and food:** Styles cards for serving, storing, faults and food pairing, and 40 quiz questions with their own *Service* filter.
- **Spot the errors:** find the lines in a tasting note that WSET says lose marks.
- **Full 25-mark written question** in the shape of WSET's sample paper, with a model answer.
- **Full theory paper:** 50 multiple-choice and four written questions under one two-hour clock.
- **Recall it:** a map drill where you type the name of the region lit up.
- **Study plan:** a study streak, an exam countdown and a daily target, kept in the backup file.
- Lazio rebuilt from the three Frascati rule books.

### 20 to 21 November
- The last unchecked facts closed from official sources: Greek Reserve ageing, Maury, Colmar's rainfall, Crémant de Bordeaux.
- The first reasoning questions for the regions that leaned most on recall.
